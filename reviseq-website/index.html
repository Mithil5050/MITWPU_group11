1. Support

About ReviseQ

ReviseQ is an engaging, AI-assisted study and revision application. It helps students learn efficiently, track their progress, earn XP, and collaborate in study groups.

How to Use the App

  • Sign In: Log in securely using your Google account to save your study progress.
  • Study & Revise: Navigate to the Study section to access smart, AI-generated revision content.
  • Track Progress: Check the Progress tab to view your learning milestones and XP earned.
  • Join Groups: Participate in study groups to learn and revise alongside your peers.

Basic Troubleshooting

  • App isn't loading: Ensure you have a stable internet connection and try restarting the app.
  • Can't sign in: Check that your Google account is active and permissions are granted.
  • Progress not saving: Ensure you are properly signed into your account so data can sync to the cloud.

Feedback & Suggestions

We are always looking to improve! If you have ideas for new features or feedback on how we can make studying better, we'd love to hear from you.

2. Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

Data Collection & Usage

At ReviseQ, we believe in transparent data practices. We collect the following information:

  • Account Information: We use Google Sign-In for secure authentication. We collect the basic profile information (like email address and name) provided by Google to create your account and save your learning progress.
  • App Usage Data: We track your study progress, XP, and group participation to provide a personalized, gamified educational experience. This data is securely stored using Supabase.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Sign-In: For secure authentication.
  • Supabase: For secure backend database and user data storage.

We do not include any third-party advertising or non-essential tracking SDKs in ReviseQ.

Local Data Handling

Some temporary data (such as cached study materials or current session progress) may be stored locally on your device to improve app performance. This data is cleared or securely synced with our servers when connected to the internet.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

ReviseQ is designed to be safe for students of all ages. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. Account creation must be done via a verified Google Account. If a parent or guardian wishes to review or delete their child's data, they can contact us directly.

Contact Us
